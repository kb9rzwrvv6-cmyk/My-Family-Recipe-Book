🍲 My Family Recipe Book

Your recipes. Your grocery lists. Your files.

Recipe library

Import a recipe from a website

Paste a recipe link. Some sites allow direct importing; others block it. If direct import fails, you can optionally try a public web relay (the URL is shared with that service), or paste the recipe page text below. Always review ingredients and quantities before saving.

The online relay is an optional third-party service, not part of your offline app. It may be unavailable or unable to read some sites.

Add a recipe

Take a photo of a handwritten card or cookbook page. The image is saved with the recipe so you can transcribe ingredients and instructions below. Automatic handwriting recognition is not included in this offline version.

Ingredients

Enter one ingredient per row. Use fractions like 1/2, 1 1/2, ½, or decimals for automatic scaling. You can leave quantity blank for “to taste.”

Weekly meal planner

Choose a recipe for each day. Add it more than once if you want to cook it twice. Grocery quantities use the servings you specify.

Grocery list

Ingredients with matching names and units are combined. Check items off as you shop. Items you already have can be checked off too.

Keep your recipes forever

Important: Recipes are stored separately on each device in the browser's local storage (which can be cleared or reach capacity, especially with many photos). They do not automatically sync to another phone or computer. Export a backup after adding recipes and keep copies somewhere safe. Clearing browser data can erase the local copy.

Import merges recipes by ID and updates matching ones. A full backup also restores meal plans and groceries. Photos are embedded in the backup, so files may be large.

Use on your phone

For iPhone Home Screen installation, host these app files on an HTTPS static site such as GitHub Pages (free). Open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it once while online to cache the app for offline use. To move data between devices: export a full JSON backup, save it in iCloud Drive, then import that file on your other device. This is manual transfer, not automatic synchronization. Each device has its own local copy; use one device as the master to avoid conflicting edits.

Privacy & offline use

No account, tracking, external libraries, or internet connection is required for the core app. URL import requires internet, and the optional online relay sends the entered URL to a third party. Everything runs in your browser. Photos stay on your device unless you export or share a backup.

Tips for scanning recipes

Photograph each card in good light, straight-on. Attach the photo and type ingredients and directions. You can also send recipe photos to ChatGPT to help transcribe them, then paste the text here. ChatGPT-based transcription requires a connection.
